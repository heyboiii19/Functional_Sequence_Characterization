BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------

The analyzed protein was initially classified as a hypothetical protein,
meaning its function was unknown.

Through sequence quality validation and homology-based analysis, the protein
was found to be similar to (S)-ureidoglycine aminohydrolase, an enzyme involved
in amino acid metabolism.

This suggests that the protein may play a role in metabolic pathways related
to amino acid degradation or utilization. Such enzymes are important for
cellular metabolism and energy balance in organisms.

This interpretation demonstrates how computational analysis can provide
functional insights for previously uncharacterized proteins.
